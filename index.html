Mohamed Siala

Senior Lecturer in Computer Science

Stockholm University

  • Combinatorial Optimisation
  • Formal Methods
  • Machine Learning
  • Artificial Intelligence

I am a computer scientist interested in various areas of computation, including algorithmic analysis, combinatorial optimisation, formal methods, reasoning, and machine learning.

I currently hold a position as a Senior Lecturer at the Department of Computer and Systems Sciences (DSV), Stockholm University. I am member of the Data Science research group at DSV. Previously, I was an Associate Professor at INSA Toulouse and a member of the ROC research group at LAAS-CNRS. Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Insight Centre for Data Analytics, University College Cork, Ireland. I completed my PhD in 2015 at LAAS-CNRS.
